- I eat the fish
	mein dha fu dhen gavahan-en
	`me (subj) fish (obj) eat (vrb)`

> The order is `Subject Object Verb (SOV)`.
> Also `Agent Subject Object Verb (ASOV)` or `Subject Object Verb Agent (SOVA)`.

- He gives me the fish
	mein dha fu dhen metegoko-thimeda
	`me (subj) fish (obj) have (3rd person male causative)`
	
	meda thiun mein dha fu dhen metegoko-en
	`him (agent) me (subj) fish (obj) have (vrb)`

> **NOTE**: There are two ways of denoting a causative verb: (1) `-thime-`, and (2) `-thiun`.
> 
> 1. `-thime-` works irregularly as a verb suffix taking the place of `en`; preceding the pronoun of the agent such as `meda`, `mein`, `memeon`. For example, `metegoko-thimeda-ksun` means that someone was made to have something by `meda` (him), in other words someone was given something by `meda` (him). When the suffix is left without a pronoun, `-thime-` works as a general agent marker, along with `-thigoko-` marking a non-human agent.
> 
> 2. `-thiun` works regularly as a particle that marks the agent (before the verb) and doesn't conjugate to the verb (so the usual `en` suffix). This is different in that it can be any noun being the agent, not only a pronoun, unlike `-thime-` which is literally made to merge with pronouns starting with "me".

- I give him the fish
	meda dha fu dhen metegoko-thimein
	`him (subj) fish (obj) have (1st person causative)`
	
	mein thiun meda dha fu dhen metegoko-en
	`me (agent) him (subj) fish (obj) have (vrb)`

- I made him move
	meda dha metan-thimein'ksun
	`him (subj) move (1st person causative, past)`
	
	mein thiun meda dha metan-en'ksun
	`me (agent) him (subj) move (past vrb)`

- Daniel made him move
	Dan'yel-theme thiun meda dha metan-en'ksun
	`Daniel-person (agent) him (subj) move (past vrb)`

- Who did it?!
	meshi thiun?!
	`who (agent/by) ?!`
	CONNOTATION: more incriminating than `dha ...`
	
	meshi dha gokoon dhen metetan-en?!
	`who (subj) it (obj) do (vrb)?!`

- Who are you?
	meon dha meshi?
	`you (subj) who ?`

- In which direction do I go?
	hometan dun mein dha metan un'dha shi?
	`direction (at) me (subj) go (clause subj) what ?`

- hair is like threads
	le un, lema dha nen-an
	`thread (context), hair (subj) similar (adj)`

- we go to there
	mein'tso dha gogun-on thaho metan-en
	`us (subj) there (to) go (vrb)`

- the fish is bigger than the human
	fu dha bon-an'bo me-thiho
	`fish (subj) big (more) person (than)`

- i kill fish and eat them because they are many
	fufu dha bo-an thi, fufu dha ksun'qa-thimein tha fufu dhen gavahan-en tha
	`fish (subj) many (adj) (because), fish (obj) kill (1st p. causative) (so) me (subj) fish (obj) eat (vrb) (so)`

- because fish are many, i eat fish
  / fish are many so i eat fish
	fufu dha bo-an thi, mein dha fufu dhen gavahan-en tha
	`fish (subj) many (adj) (because), me (subj) fish (obj) eat (vrb) (so)`

- i eat fish, because fish are many
	mein dha fufu dhen gavahan-en tha, fufu dha bo-an thi
	`me (subj) fish (obj) eat (vrb) (so), fish (subj) many (adj) (because)`

- despite fish are many, i did not see fish
	fufu dha bo-an thikso, mein dha fufu dhen mi-en'ksun'kso (tha)
	`fish (subj) many (adj) (despite), me (subj) fish (obj) see (neg. past vrb) [so]`

- i eat vegetables but i don't love them
	mein dha vava dhen gavahan-en ksa vava dhen meqan-en'kso
	`me (subj) plants (obj) eat (vrb) but plants (obj) love (neg. vrb)`

- the fish is in the sea
	fu dha in pi lugun dun
	`fish (subj) inside (of) sea (at)`

- the bird is flying in the sky
	ri dha hewi dun metan'teri-en
	`bird (subj) sky (at) fly (vrb)`

- out of all, this fish is the biggest
	ko pi fu un, fu pe gokoin dha bon-an'boko
	`all (of) fish (context), fish (adj->) this (subj) big (the most)`

- i became big / i grew
	mein dha bon-an thaho ksun'zhin-en'ksun
	`me (subj) big (adj) (towards) become (past vrb)`

- i started eating fruit
	mein dha van dhen gavahan-en gen-en'ksun
	`me (subj) fruit (obj) eat (vrb) start (past vrb)`

- if i go there i die
	mein dha gogun'on thaho metan-en un, mein dha ksun'qa-en
	`me (subj) there (to) move (if), me (subj) die (vrb)`

- the man who died eating a fish told people that he loves fish
	da pidha fu dhen gavahan-en un ksun'qa-en'ksun dha, me thaho ja-en'ksun tho "mein dha fu dhen meqan-en"
	`man (who) fish (obj) eat (vrb) die (past vrb) (subj), people (to) tell (past vrb) (quote") me (subj) fish (obj) love (vrb)

- the fish was eaten
	fu dhen gavahan-en'ksun
	`fish (obj) eat (past vrb)`

- i have a good bond with that guy / as for me, a good bond exists with that man
	da pe gokoon oun mein un, lekome pe we dha dun-en
	`man (adj->) that (with), me (context) bond (adj->) good (subj) exist (vrb)`

- stealing from people is bad
	hya pi memeon oun'kso un, me thiho-metegoko dha ksu
	`will (of) them (without) (context), person (from) taking (subj) bad`

- I can do this
	mein dha gokoin dhen toun metetan-en
	`me (subj) this (obj) can do (vrb)`
