*WIP*

In a similar fashion to the real-world parallel: the Chinese language's origins, Sen'fi is a language thought to be inspired by a god; and a set of glyphs and sounds made from the ancestors who drew what they saw and recorded what they heard. These ancestors were philosophers in the society who discovered this language (unnamed for now, perhaps "Sect of the Truth Seekers" in Sen'fi). They took the role of making a glyph and a sound for every meaning they came across from natural observation.

Sometimes the philosophers used divination when they couldn't think of a good design: they drew a lot of prototypes on a platform made of (unnamed) weak material and waited for the elements to decide which design stands untouched.

This observation and divination tradition has survived to this very day, where the (unnamed) society is still engaged in their philosophical quest to collect *logos* (understanding of the world; words and reasoning).

However, there is a catch: the god once told them that if they want to please it, they must avoid:
1. making complex sounds, as that would resemble the babbling of their neighbouring barbarians
2. using the same sound for multiple separate meanings, as that would offend the sacred atomicity of words
3. making arbitrary sounds and glyphs, as that would collect *logos* poorly

As the society collected *logos*, they became more capable of communication and intellectual advancement. Their needs shifted from simple and physiological such as food and shelter, to complex and transcendental, such as the expression of human emotion and the drive to discover truth.
