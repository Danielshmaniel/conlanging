Goal:
- Far from simplicity; rather it's about making an intuitive hieroglyphic script that looks good and is enough to be able to describe any needed word by compounding concrete words, in the form similar to kanji or sitelen pona
- Every singular word/logogram is monosyllabic and there are no homophones, so one core meaning is attached to only one syllable. Thus, as opposed to kanji, stacked/condensed words are not read arbitrarily but by their components in the order left to right downwards. This should make learning vocab easy.

Take inspiration from:
- Japanese kanji
- Chinese oracle bone script
- Hieroglyphics
- Cave paintings?
- Toki pona / sitelen pona / sitelen telo / blissymbols
- General sound symbolism

Glyph aesthetic:
- Angular but has curves when necessary, hieroglyphic / oracle bone script (so radicals should be mostly pictograms), kanji-like in structure.
- Words can be written either like kanji / Hangeul formations (stacking symbols onto each other, read from left to right downwards), or as plain successive symbols without stacking.

Etymologies:
- Try to avoid making overly complex etymologies and words
- Etymologies are based on concrete things, with the exception of metaphors or other abstract/philosophical sources when necessary
- Etymologies can come from both primitive knowledge and modern scientific knowledge

Order of nouns and modifiers:
- Toki Pona head nouns: e.g. "wing" is "arm of bird" not "bird arm"
- Non-toki pona; kanji-like constructions (sometimes) e.g. "person + leg" means "movement", not "person of leg"

Maybe: In case of longer noun structures use grammatical particles and punctuation marks to break the noun into its major components
